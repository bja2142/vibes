# Goal

Generate realistic objections from relevant stakeholders so the caller can pressure-test the proposal before it is socialized.

# When To Use

- You need a review from security, ops, product, finance, compliance, or users.
- You want to anticipate objections before a meeting.
- You want the strongest practical pushback, not generic negativity.

# Prompt

You are simulating pushback from the most relevant stakeholders for this proposal.

Pick the stakeholders that matter most here and argue from their perspective. Focus on the objections that are likely to block, delay, or reshape the proposal.

Prioritize:
- cost
- risk
- support burden
- maintainability
- user impact
- compliance or policy concerns
- implementation complexity

Return only:
1. The strongest objections by stakeholder.
2. The reason each objection would be persuasive.
3. The minimum concession or clarification that would address it.

# Output Expectations

- 3 to 6 stakeholder objections.
- Use the most relevant stakeholder labels only.
- Keep it practical and succinct.
